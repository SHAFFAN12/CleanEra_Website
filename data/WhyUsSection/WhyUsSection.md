# Component: `WhyUsSection`

A clean, centered section that highlights the industries CleanEra serves. It consists of a **centered heading**, a **horizontal row of 4 industry cards**, and a **centered CTA button** below the cards. Background is plain white throughout.

---

## Overall Section Structure (Top to Bottom)

1. **Section Heading** — centered, two-line bold title
2. **4-Column Industry Card Row** — equally spaced cards in a single horizontal row
3. **CTA Button** — centered below the cards

---

## 1. Section Heading

- **Text:**
  - Line 1: `Trusted Across Multiple`
  - Line 2: `Industries`
- **Alignment:** Horizontally **center-aligned**.
- **Font:** Bold / heavy weight sans-serif.
- **Font size:** Approximately **36–42px**.
- **Color:** Near-black / dark navy — `#0D1B2A` or `#111111`.
- **Line height:** ~1.2–1.3 (tight).
- **Letter spacing:** Normal.
- **Margin bottom:** ~40–56px before the card row.
- **No subheading, no decorative element** under this heading.
- **Background:** White `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 2. Four-Column Industry Card Row

### Row Layout
- **Number of cards:** 4, arranged in a **single horizontal row**.
- **Layout type:** Equal-width columns — each card takes up ~25% of the container width.
- **Gap between cards:** ~16–20px.
- **Container:** Max-width centered container with horizontal padding of ~60–80px on each side.
- **Vertical alignment:** All 4 cards are the same height — top-aligned with each other.

---

### Individual Card Styling (All Cards — Common Properties)

- **Background:** White `#FFFFFF`.
- **Border:** Thin light grey border — approximately `1px solid #E0E0E0` or `#EBEBEB`. Very subtle, barely visible.
- **Border radius:** ~12–16px on all four corners.
- **Box shadow:** Very subtle — soft light grey shadow. Approximately `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` or similar. The shadow is barely noticeable — cards appear almost flat with just a very faint lift.
- **Padding:** ~24–28px on all sides (top, right, bottom, left).
- **Height:** Approximately **260–290px** tall (all cards equal height).
- **Width:** Equal — approximately **240–270px** each (or ~23–24% of container).

### Card Internal Layout (Top to Bottom)

Each card has this vertical structure:
1. **Number label** (top-left corner)
2. **Illustration icon** (top-right area, same row as number)
3. **Industry title** (below icon, center-aligned)
4. **Description paragraph** (below title, center-aligned)

---

### A. Number Label

- **Position:** Top-left corner of the card.
- **Text:** `01`, `02`, `03`, `04` — two-digit zero-padded numbers.
- **Font:** Regular or medium weight sans-serif.
- **Font size:** Approximately **14–16px**.
- **Color:** **Light sky blue / cornflower blue** — approximately `#6BAED6`, `#5B9BD5`, or `#87BFDF`. Clearly blue, muted/medium saturation. NOT the dark navy, NOT the brand green — a distinct light blue.
- **Alignment:** Top-left corner — flush with the card's internal padding (top-left after padding).
- **No background, no badge** — plain text only.

---

### B. Illustration Icon

- **Position:** Top-right area of the card, on the same horizontal level as the number label. The icon sits to the right while the number is to the left — they share the same top row but are on opposite ends.
- **Size:** Approximately **60–72px** tall × **60–72px** wide (square bounding box).
- **Style:** **Flat colorful illustration / icon** — multi-color, cartoon/isometric-lite style. These are NOT simple line icons or monochrome icons — they are full-color illustrated icons with multiple colors.
- **No background circle or badge** behind the icon — the illustration floats directly on the white card background.

#### Icon Details Per Card:

**Card 1 — Corporate Offices:**
- Icon: A **document/paper with lines of text** and a **play button (orange/amber triangle)** overlaid on top of it — resembling a video or media document icon.
- Colors: White document with grey lines, **bright amber/orange triangle play button** in the center.
- The overall feel: a document with a video-play overlay.
- Approximate palette: Orange `#F5A623` / `#FF9900`, grey `#CCCCCC`, white `#FFFFFF`.

**Card 2 — Warehouses:**
- Icon: A **group of colorful books / file folders** stacked or standing upright side by side.
- Colors: Multiple colors — **red**, **green**, **yellow/orange** — like a collection of colorful books or binders lined up on a shelf. Each "book" is a different color.
- Approximate palette: Red `#E74C3C`, Green `#27AE60`, Yellow/Orange `#F39C12`.
- The feel: a library shelf or filing cabinet icon.

**Card 3 — Manufacturing Plants:**
- Icon: A **calendar/planner** with a **green checkmark badge** in the lower-right corner of the icon.
- The calendar is styled with:
  - **Blue/teal header** at the top (typical calendar month header area).
  - **Red binding rings** at the top.
  - A **grid of small squares** in the body (representing calendar dates) — some squares are colored/highlighted in teal/blue.
  - A **green circle with a white checkmark** (`✓`) overlaid at the bottom-right of the calendar.
- Colors: Teal/blue `#3498DB`, red `#E74C3C`, green `#2ECC71`, white `#FFFFFF`.

**Card 4 — Medical Clinics:**
- Icon: A **document/clipboard** with a **pen or pencil** and what appears to be **a small flag or marker** — resembling a checklist or form being filled out.
- Colors: **Salmon/coral-orange** document body with **teal/blue** accent details. A pencil or marker in a dark color overlaid diagonally.
- Approximate palette: Coral/salmon `#E8A090` or `#F4A080`, teal `#2980B9` or `#5DADE2`.
- The feel: a form or medical record being documented.

---

### C. Industry Title

- **Position:** Centered below the icon, after a vertical gap of ~20–28px from the icon.
- **Font:** Bold / semi-bold sans-serif.
- **Font size:** Approximately **16–18px**.
- **Color:** Dark navy / near-black — `#0D1B2A` or `#1A1A2E`.
- **Alignment:** **Center-aligned** within the card.
- **Line wrapping:** Some titles wrap to 2 lines:
  - Card 1: `Corporate` / `Offices` (2 lines)
  - Card 2: `Warehouses` (1 line)
  - Card 3: `Manufacturing` / `Plants` (2 lines)
  - Card 4: `Medical Clinics` (1 line)
- **Margin bottom:** ~10–14px before description.

---

### D. Description Paragraph

- **Text (all cards — placeholder/same text):**
  > *"We are experienced in bringing adventures to their journey, with all outdoor destinations."*
  *(Note: This is clearly placeholder/lorem-ipsum-style text. The real final copy should be supplied by the client for each card.)*
- **Font:** Regular weight sans-serif.
- **Font size:** Approximately **12–14px**.
- **Color:** Medium grey — `#888888` or `#999999` (light grey, clearly secondary).
- **Alignment:** **Center-aligned** within the card.
- **Line height:** ~1.5–1.6 (comfortable reading).
- **Width:** Full card width minus padding — text naturally wraps to 3 lines within the card.
- **No "Read More" or link below** — description is the final element in each card.

## 3. CTA Button

- **Position:** Horizontally **centered** below the card row.
- **Margin top from cards:** ~40–52px.
- **Text:** `Book An Appointment`
- **Background:** Light sky blue / baby blue — `#87CEEB` or `#6EC6F5` (same brand CTA blue used throughout the site).
- **Text color:** White `#FFFFFF`.
- **Font:** Regular or semi-bold sans-serif. ~14–15px.
- **Border radius:** **Pill/capsule** shape — `~50px` border-radius (fully rounded ends).
- **Padding:** ~12–14px vertical × ~40–48px horizontal.
- **Width:** Approximately **220–240px** (fits text with generous padding).
- **No border/stroke.** Flat style — no visible drop shadow.
- **Alignment:** Centered within the full section width.

## Icon Assets Required

All 4 icons are **flat colorful illustrations** — multi-color, slightly playful/isometric-lite style. They should be sourced or designed as SVG or PNG with transparent background.

1. **Card 1:** Document/paper with orange play-button triangle overlay.
2. **Card 2:** Colorful books or file folders standing upright (red, green, yellow/orange).
3. **Card 3:** Calendar with green checkmark badge (teal calendar grid, red rings, green check circle).
4. **Card 4:** Coral/salmon document or clipboard with teal pen or pencil marker.

> These can be sourced from icon libraries such as **Flaticon**, **Icons8**, **Freepik**, or **LottieFiles** using search terms like "corporate office icon flat", "warehouse books icon flat", "calendar checkmark icon flat", "medical clipboard icon flat".

---

## Responsive Notes

- On **tablet (2-column):** Stack cards into a 2×2 grid — 2 cards per row.
- On **mobile (1-column):** Stack all 4 cards vertically — 1 card per row, full-width.
- Heading remains centered at all breakpoints.
- CTA button stays centered; may go full-width on mobile.
- Card icons remain same size across breakpoints; card padding reduces slightly on mobile (~16–20px).
